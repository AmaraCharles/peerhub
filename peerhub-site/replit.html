Frequently asked questions

Everything clear before you begin.

Quick answers about your money, your card, and the everyday payments you can make with PeerHub. Can't find what you're after? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out.

What can I use PeerHub for? +

Buy and hold crypto, pay utility bills, send money, and spend with a PeerHub virtual card from one account. It's built to replace the handful of separate apps most people use for everyday money.

How quickly are bill payments processed? +

Most supported payments are confirmed in seconds. You receive an in-app confirmation as soon as the provider accepts the payment, so you're never left guessing whether it went through.

Can I use the virtual card online? +

Yes. Use your virtual card for online shopping and subscriptions, while keeping control of your limits and spending. You can freeze it, adjust limits, or top it up instantly from the app.

How does the WhatsApp assistant work? +

Ask the assistant to help with supported crypto, bill, and transfer tasks directly from the WhatsApp conversation you already use. It's available around the clock and picks up right where you left off.

Is my money and crypto safe with PeerHub? +

Yes. Funds are held with regulated partners, transactions are encrypted end-to-end, and every account is protected with multi-factor authentication. Card and crypto activity can also be monitored and frozen instantly from the app.

Which countries and currencies does PeerHub support? +

PeerHub currently supports a growing list of countries and local currencies alongside major cryptocurrencies and stablecoins. Open the app and check your region's settings for the exact list available to you.

Do I need to verify my identity to use PeerHub? +

Yes. A quick identity check is required to keep your account and funds secure and to comply with local regulations. Most verifications take just a few minutes and unlock full access to crypto, bills, and card features.

What fees does PeerHub charge? +

PeerHub keeps fees simple and transparent — you'll always see the exact cost before you confirm a crypto trade, bill payment, or transfer, with no hidden charges added afterward.

What if I need help outside the WhatsApp assistant? +

Our support team is reachable in-app and by email for anything the assistant can't resolve, including account and card issues. Most enquiries get a response within a few hours.
