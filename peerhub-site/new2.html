Support

Frequently asked questions.

Everything about converting, paying bills, your card, and chatting with our WhatsApp AI. Still stuck? Just ask in the app.

What is PeerHub? +

PeerHub is an app that lets you convert crypto to fiat and back, pay your utility bills, and spend with a virtual dollar card — all in one place.

How does crypto-to-fiat conversion work? +

Add crypto to your PeerHub balance and convert it to naira or dollars at the live rate — no hidden markups. You can also go the other way and convert your fiat balance back into crypto.

What bills can I pay on PeerHub? +

Electricity, airtime, mobile data, and cable TV subscriptions — paid straight from your PeerHub balance, crypto or fiat.

What is the virtual dollar card? +

It’s a virtual USD card you fund instantly from your PeerHub balance, for subscriptions, online shopping, ads, and anything else that accepts Visa or Mastercard.

How do I fund my virtual card? +

Convert crypto or fiat straight into your card balance from the app — it’s ready to spend immediately, no waiting on approvals.

What can the WhatsApp AI actually do? +

Message PeerHub on WhatsApp and the AI can convert currency, pay a bill, check your balance, or send money for you — no need to open the app.

Is my money and my crypto safe on PeerHub? +

Yes. Every transaction is encrypted, and all users are verified before they can convert, pay bills, or spend.
